## L1: payout ratio and UC gain conditions

**Why this matters.** For an accepted matrix, payout changes can be computed exactly. Each
listener’s weight is their share of a particular artist’s plays. An
unweighted average over people cannot replace this calculation.

**What it says.** The matrix, or its artist-specific weighted audience means, is needed to
determine the winner. Niche status, audience size or heterogeneity alone
is insufficient. Both payouts and their common budget are checked after
accepting a matrix; the desired direction of payout redistribution is
not an optimization target.
